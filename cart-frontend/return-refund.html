When can I return an item?

We accept returns on eligible products that meet our return conditions. Please make sure the item is in acceptable condition before submitting a request.

FAQ

Returns & refunds questions

How do I start a return? +

Contact CART Support with your order number, the item involved and the reason for your request.

When will I receive my refund? +

After an approved return has been reviewed, the refund is processed through the applicable payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.

Can I exchange an item? +

An exchange may be possible for eligible products depending on stock availability.

What if I received the wrong product? +

Contact Support with your order details so we can help resolve the issue.

What if my item arrived damaged? +

Contact Support promptly and provide your order details so the issue can be reviewed.

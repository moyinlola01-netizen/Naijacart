Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains the information Cart collects and how it is used.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

When you create an account or place an order, Cart may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery information.

We may also collect information about products you add to your cart, orders you place, and your interactions with the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Cart may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process and manage your orders.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve the shopping experience.
  • Maintain the security of the platform.

3. Account Information

If you create an account, you are responsible for keeping your login information private and secure.

Passwords are stored using secure password hashing rather than being stored as plain text.

4. Orders and Delivery

Information such as your name, phone number, email address, and delivery address may be required to process and deliver an order.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Cart may use browser technologies such as local storage to remember certain information, including shopping preferences and authentication information.

You can manage browser storage through your browser settings.

6. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account and order information from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Information Sharing

Personal information should only be shared when necessary to provide requested services, operate the platform, comply with applicable requirements, or protect the security of Cart and its users.

8. Your Choices

You may contact Cart support if you have questions about your account information or need assistance with your account.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Cart platform or its practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

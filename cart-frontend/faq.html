How do I shop on CART?

Browse the Shop page, search for products, open a product to view its details, select available options and add it to your cart. When you're ready, proceed to checkout.

02

How do I search for a product?

Use the search box on the Shop page and enter the name or description of the product you are looking for.

03
🏷️

Can I filter products by category?

Yes. Products can be browsed by categories such as Electronics, Fashion, Home and Beauty.

04

Can I see product ratings and reviews?

Yes. Product pages can display ratings, review counts and customer feedback where those details are available.

05

How do I add a product to my cart?

Open the product you want and select the appropriate options, such as size when available. Then select Add to Cart.

06
➕

Can I change the quantity in my cart?

Yes. Open your cart and use the quantity controls beside the product you want to update.

07
🗑️

Can I remove an item from my cart?

Yes. Select the remove option beside the product you no longer want in your cart.

08

Will my cart items remain after refreshing?

Cart items are stored locally so your cart can remain available after refreshing the page on the same browser.
